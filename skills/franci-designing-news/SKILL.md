## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 1. Designer as Information Architect (Ch 01)
Editorial designers are not drawing-board stylists who decorate articles after they are written. Designers are journalists and information architects who organize news complexity into clear visual logic, structured spatial grids, and intuitive reading pathways.

### 2. The Modular Storytelling Architecture Model (Ch 02)
Deconstruct long narrative articles ($>1,500$ words) into six integrated visual layers:
$$\text{Main Text Column} + \text{Hero Visual} + \text{Big Data Callout} + \text{Explanatory Diagram} + \text{Timeline Sidebar} + \text{Page Furniture}$$
This provides multiple scannable entry points for different reader engagement levels.

### 3. The Structural Redesign Matrix (Ch 03)
A publication redesign is a structural overhaul executed across four interdependent layers:
- **Editorial Strategy**: Target personas, section architecture, content pacing.
- **Information Grid**: Rebuilding master 6-column or 12-column grids for multi-span flexibility.
- **Typographic Identity**: Commissioning bespoke font super-families (e.g., *Guardian Egyptian*).
- **Newsroom Workflow**: Adapting CMS tools and templates to streamline daily production under deadline pressure.

### 4. Infographic Thinking & The Double-Page Data Spread (Ch 04)
Translate complex news phenomena into structured visual maps using Francesco Franci's *IL* magazine spread methodology:
- **Hero Macro Anchor**: A large central visual diagram depicting the core phenomenon.
- **Micro Satellite Callouts**: 4 to 6 detailed sub-charts and text captions surrounding the macro visual.
- **Unified Typographic Palette**: 1 crisp sans-serif font family in small weights ($8\text{pt}\text{--}10\text{pt}$).
- **Semantic Color Coding**: Assign 2 to 3 colors strictly to categorize data; maintain color consistency across all charts.

### 5. The Tri-Screen News Convergence Model (Ch 05)
Deliver a unified news brand experience across channels:
- **Print Edition**: Fixed spatial format, high-resolution physical spreads, tactile pacing, deep weekend reading.
- **Desktop & Web**: Continuous vertical scroll, fluid 12-column grid breakpoints, instant engagement.
- **Tablet & Mobile Apps**: Touch interactivity, dual-orientation shifts (portrait single-column vs. landscape multi-column), scrollytelling multimedia features.

### 6. The Editorial Innovation Benchmark Model (Ch 06)
Analyze breakthrough news design benchmarks:
- **Francesco Franci (*IL*)**: Infographic-driven editorial design & data spreads.
- **Richard Turley (*Bloomberg Businessweek*)**: Irreverent, meme-aware, high-impact display typography.
- **Mark Porter (*The Guardian*)**: Berliner format redesign & bespoke *Guardian Egyptian* super-family.
- **Steve Duenes (*New York Times: Snow Fall*)**: Interactive scrollytelling & multimedia integration.
- **Oliver Reichenstein (*Zeit Online*)**: Responsive web typography & engineering excellence under deadline pressure.
