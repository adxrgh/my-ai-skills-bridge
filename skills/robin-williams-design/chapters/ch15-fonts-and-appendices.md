# Chapter 15: 本书所用字体 (Fonts Used in This Book) & Appendices

## Core Idea
This final chapter documents the 300+ typefaces used throughout the book, organized by category, with page references. It also includes a mini glossary, resources, and interviews with the author.

## Key Content
### Font List (by category):
- **Body text**: 方正书宋简体 (Founder's Book Song Simplified), 10/16pt (10pt font, 16pt leading).
- **Modern (现代体)**: 14pt samples.
- **Slab serif (粗衬线体)**: 14pt samples (Clarendon, Memphis, etc.).
- **Sans serif (无衬线体)**: 13pt samples (Eurostile, Formata, Syntax, Frutiger, Myriad, Avenir, etc.).
- **Oldstyle (旧式体)**: 14pt samples (Garamond, Jenson, Caslon, Minion, Palatino, etc.).
- **Decorative (花体)**: 18pt samples.
- **Script (手写体)**: 14pt samples.

### Mini Glossary (迷你术语表):
- **Baseline (基线)**: the invisible line beneath the font.
- **Body text (正文)**: main reading text, typically 9–12pt.
- **Ornament (装饰标志)**: a small decorative character (e.g., bullets, dingbats like Zapf Dingbats or Wingdings).

### Resources:
- **CreativeMarket.com**: "designer's Etsy" — cheap fonts, templates, images.
- **MyFonts.com**: affordable fonts.
- **Font Squirrel.com**: free and affordable fonts.

### Author Interview (附录B — 图灵访谈):
- **Teaching experience**: Robin started teaching in the 1970s; the book grew out of her classroom workbook.
- **"Why is your book easy to understand?"**: she wrote it imagining herself teaching in a classroom; the conversational tone came from answering students' real questions.
- **"Type and Life" connection**: the contrast/harmony/conflict framework applies to relationships — you can create conflict (painful), harmony (nice but boring), or healthy contrast (meaningful debate, adventure, growth). Type is the same.
- **The 4 principles**: she didn't "invent" them — she extracted them from analyzing thousands of designs. They're universal (apply to Chinese characters too).
- **Recommended reading**: *The Non-Designer's Type Book*; *The Non-Designer's InDesign/Photoshop/Illustrator Books*; Communication Arts annuals (browse, find what you love, describe why).
- **On infographics**: a good visualization transforms data into understanding; the CRAP principles apply to information graphics too.
- **Current project**: pursuing a PhD on the Shakespeare authorship question.

## Key Takeaways
1. You only need a few well-chosen type families — one from each category with a good weight range.
2. The 4 principles are universal across languages (Chinese, English, etc.).
3. Learning to describe WHY a design works is the path to creating your own.
4. Recommended resources: CreativeMarket, MyFonts, Font Squirrel for fonts; Communication Arts annuals for inspiration.
5. Robin's extension books: *The Non-Designer's Type Book*, *InDesign Book*, *Photoshop Book*, *Illustrator Book*.
