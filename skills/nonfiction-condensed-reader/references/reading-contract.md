# Nonfiction reading-edition contract

The output must read continuously as a shorter version of the book's reasoning, not as chapter notes, a review, or a generic list of takeaways.

## Opening map

State the central thesis, the problem being challenged, the main sections of the argument, and only the minimum key terms needed to begin. Opening claims must use facts marked `orientation_safe`.

## Argument units

Group source chapters only when they form one natural argumentative move. Preserve source order and chapter IDs as location metadata. Explain claims, evidence, counterarguments, examples, and implications in connected prose.

Compress repetition, transitions, citation inventories, and examples that do not change understanding. Do not remove qualifications, uncertainty, limits, or evidence that materially constrain a claim.

Each unit ends with `why_it_matters`, phrased as the consequence for understanding or practice rather than praise of the author.

## Canonical passages

The condensed explanation must stand on its own without inserted passages. A passage earns its place only for exact formulation, reasoning, example detail, method, or voice that paraphrase would materially flatten.

`lead_in` may cite only facts supported before the passage begins. `takeaway` may cite facts supported by its end. The plan must move forward through source blocks without gaps or backward jumps.

## Closing synthesis

Summarize the argument backbone, myths and revisions, key concepts, evidence and cases, practice implications, open questions or limits, and a rereading map. Distinguish the author's claims from the condenser's interpretation.

Keep IDs, scores, hashes, and validation details in sidecars rather than reading prose.
