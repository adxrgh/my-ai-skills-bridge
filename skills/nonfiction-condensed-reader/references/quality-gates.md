# Quality gates

Run deterministic verification before judging the prose.

## Deterministic gates

- Every canonical block belongs to exactly one batch's selectable range.
- Every batch has one valid analysis file.
- Every fact and window ID is unique and resolves to the indexed source.
- Window bounds are ordered and local; decisive blocks are selectable.
- Plan claims obey their support boundary and units cover the source continuously.
- Every rendered source passage exactly matches its canonical span and SHA-256.
- No model-authored JSON contains source prose or a long copied-source match.

## Reading gates

1. Remove all source passages mentally. The remaining prose must still communicate the central thesis, the tenets being challenged, the reasoning, important evidence, qualifications, and implications.
2. Restore the passages. Each must add precision, reasoning texture, example detail, method, or voice that the explanation cannot replace.
3. Remove passages selected merely because their conclusions are important.
4. Check that repeated examples were compressed without deleting materially different evidence.
5. Keep claims, evidence, examples, and the condenser's implications distinguishable.
6. Confirm that the closing synthesis represents the whole book, including limits and open questions, rather than only memorable chapters.

Do not expose internal scores, fact IDs, or checklist language in the final reading prose.
