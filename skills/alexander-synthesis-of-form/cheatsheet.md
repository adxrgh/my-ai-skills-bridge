# Synthesis of Form - Decision Sheet

## Start Here

| If you see... | Do... | Because... |
|---|---|---|
| A vague quality such as “simple” or “delightful” | Ask what observable form-context failure would contradict it | Misfits are more operational than total descriptions of goodness |
| More requirements than one person can integrate | Externalize variables and interactions before drawing the whole | Complexity lies in coupling, not just count |
| A clean category tree from departments or topics | Test whether actual interactions stay inside its branches | Verbal coherence is not functional independence |
| A derived cluster with strange neighbors | Delay naming; seek shared physical implications | Surprise may be the main discovery |
| Constant cross-team renegotiation | Move the boundary toward a sparser interaction cut | Good subsystems contain most consequences locally |
| A module that works alone but breaks the whole | Reconcile at its parent and revise the hierarchy if needed | Local fit is insufficient for ensemble fit |

## Workflow

1. **Boundary** - What is controllable form? What context makes demands?
2. **Misfits** - What specific relations can go wrong?
3. **Coverage** - Include felt needs, external obligations, and implicit successes.
4. **Normalize** - Make variables comparable in scope; remove solution wording.
5. **Interactions** - Does correcting A help, hinder, or not affect B? Record evidence and confidence.
6. **Decompose** - Find strong internal coupling and weak external coupling; recurse.
7. **Diagram** - For each set, express all required physical relations and no arbitrary detail.
8. **Compose** - Combine siblings upward; inspect boundary conflicts.
9. **Recheck** - If no coherent diagram emerges, revise variables or links.
10. **Prototype** - Put form back into context; observed fit outranks the model.

## Diagram Acceptance Test

| Test | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Requirement completeness | Every variable in the active set is addressed |
| Relevance | No feature lacks a reason in the active set |
| Generality | It represents a family of fitting forms, not one arbitrary styling |
| Physical specificity | Spatial/material relations are concrete enough to test |
| Composability | It can join parent and sibling diagrams without destroying their fit |

## Fast Smells

- A requirement names a component: likely a seeded solution.
- One vertex links to nearly everything: scope may be too broad.
- Many duplicate edges arise from shared vocabulary: interactions may be semantic, not causal.
- All clusters match existing departments: decomposition may have learned the organization chart.
- A diagram has brand, decoration, or finish decisions too early: arbitrary detail has entered.
- The model says “independent,” but implementation teams coordinate constantly: missing links or wrong boundary.
- Redesign removes an old nuisance and also an unnoticed benefit: implicit successes were omitted.

## Non-Negotiable Judgments

- The program organizes attention; it does not generate the answer.
- The form-context ensemble, not the artifact alone, is the unit of evaluation.
- Pairwise mathematics is an aid with simplifying assumptions, not an oracle.
- Preserve intuition for physical synthesis while making its inputs criticizable.
